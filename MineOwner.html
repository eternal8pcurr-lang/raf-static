01
Property Assessment
Working with Geo Mineral Science

The process begins with a thorough evaluation of your property by Geo Mineral Science — our Stage 1 coordination partner for geological assessment and certification preparation.

  • Geo Mineral Science reviews your property and assesses its potential for the RealGold program.
  • Third-party geologists and laboratories are contracted to prepare the property for certification.
  • Initial assaying, sampling, and geological coordination are completed under independent oversight.
  • All properties must demonstrate continuous holding with an unbroken chain of title.
02
Certification & Preparation
Meeting NI 43-101 and 43-99 Standards

Once the initial assessment is complete, the property moves into full certification — the foundation for everything that follows.

  • Verified reports are prepared to NI 43-101 standards by qualified independent geologists.
  • RAF's own 43-99 certification provides an additional layer of validation and due diligence.
  • Chain of title, mineral rights, and continuous holding are confirmed and documented.
  • The property is now market-ready — certified, documented, and prepared for trust formation.

03
Tripartite Estate & Mineral Trust
Separating, Protecting, and Organizing Your Property
  • The Tripartite Declaration separates your estate into three parts: mineral, biological, and social.
  • The mineral estate conveys Earth Blocks into a bioregional mineral trust.
  • You remain the mine owner and trustee — your property stays in your care and under your stewardship.
  • By forming the trust, you agree to the next step: bringing the certified mineral estate to market.
04
Bringing It to Market
DST and RealGold Token — Two Channels, One Asset

Your certified mineral real property enters two markets simultaneously — creating liquidity, stability, and ongoing value.

▲

Your Certified Mineral Estate

Earth Blocks registered on the Real Assets Registry — Holochain-based, tamper-evident, fully auditable.

Channel 1 — DST

Delaware Statutory Trust for §1031 exchange investors. Regulated, broker-dealer facilitated, perpetual hold.

Channel 2 — Token

RealGold Token for digital markets. Same certified asset, same Registry, broader market access.

You're not selling your mine. You're monetizing its non-extractive value through two market channels — while the gold stays in the ground and your property stays protected.

Getting Paid
First Lien Priority — You Come First

Revenue from both DST and token markets flows through a clear priority structure. Your agreed-upon payment comes before anything else.

1st
Mine Owner Payment — First lien on all DST and token revenue pays your agreed-upon amount. You get paid before stewardship, before distributions, before anything else moves.
2nd
Compliance & Operations — Trust administration, regulatory compliance, Registry maintenance, and market coordination.
3rd
Stewardship & Distributions — Environmental reclamation, social programs, and investor distributions from remaining proceeds.
06
Stewardship & Ongoing Participation
Your Property Becomes a Regenerative Asset

Once your lien is satisfied, the stewardship program activates — and your property begins its next chapter.

A regenerative vision: solar energy, greenhouses, animal husbandry, earth buildings, and restored land — funded by the RealGold stewardship program.
  • You choose your level of involvement — active participant or hands-off, under the Real Assets Foundation stewardship framework.
  • Minerals stay permanently in situ, stabilizing both DST and token markets while funding environmental reclamation and regenerative programs.
  • Parts of your mine can continue operating without encumbrance, as long as all activity aligns with RAF and the stewardship framework.

This is the long game. Your gold stays in the ground. Your property heals and grows. Your payment comes first. And the asset you've spent years developing becomes the foundation for something that lasts generations — not just for you, but for the land and the communities around it.
